# Responsible Use (שימוש אחראי)

## Lottery draws are independent

Every lottery draw is an **independent random event**. The outcome of past draws
does not influence the probability of future draws.

## What Statistiloto shows

Statistiloto provides **historical observations** — how often numbers and groups
have appeared in past draws. This is descriptive statistics, not predictive
statistics.

- ✅ Correct: "Number 17 appeared 45 times in the last 200 draws."
- ✅ Correct: "The pair 17+31 is the most frequent pair in the archive."
- ❌ Wrong: "Number 17 is due to appear soon."
- ❌ Wrong: "The pair 17+31 has a higher chance of being drawn next time."
- ❌ Wrong: "Cold numbers are overdue."

## What the AI will never say

The AI assistant is programmed to never describe historical frequency as improved
winning probability. When presenting statistics, it includes a disclaimer:

- Hebrew: אלה נתוני עבר בלבד; הם לא מעידים שלקבוצה מסוימת יש סיכוי גבוה יותר בהגרלה הבאה.
- English: These are historical observations only and do not imply a higher
  probability in the next draw.

## Enjoy responsibly

Statistiloto is a lottery intelligence tool for exploring historical patterns.
It is not a prediction tool, not a guarantee, and not financial advice. Play
responsibly.
